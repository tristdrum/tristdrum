# Cleaning-Team Authority

The cleaner report communicates which rooms need work and the verified guest
timing. It does not decide who is working. These rules also apply to Codex
heartbeats, support operations, and ad hoc WhatsApp messages.

## Attendance and Temporary Cover

- Do not recruit, book, invite, confirm attendance, set a worker's start time,
  extend temporary cover, cancel shifts, or promise work or pay without Tristan's
  specific authorization for that worker, date, and arrangement.
- A verified existing owner instruction for that person and date can be relayed
  faithfully. A previous shift, recurring room work, group membership, or a
  worker asking to come is not authorization for a new shift.
- Temporary cover ends with the explicitly arranged dates. Do not extrapolate a
  recurring schedule from a one-off engagement.
- Identify the actual sender using provider participant/message identity and
  the matching contact or direct conversation. An empty display name is unknown
  identity, not a generic message from the regular cleaning team. A name in the
  message body may be an addressee rather than the sender.
- Read the relevant group and direct-message context for owner instructions and
  later corrections. If identity or authorization is missing or contradictory,
  make no staffing commitment; surface the question privately to Tristan once.

Continue authorized bilingual English/isiXhosa room plans, guest timing notes,
and readiness checks without adding invitations or promises that an individual
should attend. Readiness evidence concerns a named room's state; it does not
establish the sender's employment schedule.

## Delivery and Corrections

Dry-run success, idempotency, and exact readback prove transport and duplicate
safety. They do not prove authority to make a staffing commitment. Check identity
and authorization before using the existing delivery helpers.

If Tristan has corrected an erroneous arrangement and the worker acknowledges
it, record the corrected state. Do not answer stale follow-up questions or send
another apology, invitation, cancellation, or reminder merely to close the
loop. Historical successful-delivery records must not become valid staffing
precedents.

## Review Examples

| Situation | Permitted behavior |
| --- | --- |
| A temporary worker asks to come tomorrow after a room plan is posted. | Check the sender and dated owner arrangement; without authorization, ask Tristan privately and make no commitment. |
| The only approved shift was last Saturday. | Treat that arrangement as completed, even if tomorrow has rooms to clean. |
| Tristan explicitly asks to tell a named worker to come on a specified date at a specified time. | Relay that exact authorized arrangement using the bilingual verified send route. |
| A group message has no display name and its sender cannot be matched. | Do not assume it came from a regular worker. |
| A named studio is reported ready. | Reconcile the room and stay under the existing readiness rules; infer no future shift. |
| Tristan has corrected an invitation and the worker accepted it. | Preserve the correction and stay quiet while unchanged. |
