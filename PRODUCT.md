# Public website

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Future partners, investors and collaborators evaluating Tristan Drummond's work and judgment.

## Product Purpose

Explain who Tristan is, what he builds, and how to start a useful professional conversation. Credibility comes from specific work and verifiable sources.

## Positioning

South African founder and technologist, based in East London. Co-founder and CTO at WorkWeek; co-founder at Tech Local. Practical software for field teams and businesses, with respect for the people using it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing React, TypeScript and Vite application; publish to the existing tristdrum.com domain through GitHub-connected Vercel deployment.
- Public portfolio at `/`; public artwork viewer at `/artwork/:id`.
- Preserve owner authentication, private dashboard functionality, password-protected financial tools and operational workers. The user permits incidental shared visual changes, but the public experience is the priority.
- No private data, financial figures, household details, raw documents, invented achievements or unsupported current project claims.
- The user delegated copy, information architecture and visual decisions for this redesign, including implementation and verified live release.

## Brand Commitments

Distinctive, credible and direct. Avoid inflated self-promotion. Human dignity, practical service and generosity inform the narrative; personal beliefs are expressed briefly and without household detail.

## Evidence on Hand

- [WorkWeek](https://www.workweek.tech/) and its [team page](https://www.workweek.tech/about): product capabilities and Tristan's co-founder/CTO role.
- [Tech Local](https://www.techlocal.co.za/) and its [about page](https://www.techlocal.co.za/about): venture building, custom software and AI services.
- [PowerSync interview, 13 September 2024](https://powersync.com/blog/developer-case-study-tristan-drummond-gideon-boshoff-workweek): Tristan and Gideon Boshoff discuss WorkWeek's offline architecture. Historical technical evidence, not a current usage metric.
- Public professional portrait from Tech Local's team page; preserve its identity and record its origin.
- The former site and repository README contained dated revenue, equity and team figures. Do not reuse them as proof.

## Product Principles

- Make the role, work and next step clear immediately.
- Let public evidence support the narrative.
- Distinguish the person from the work of the wider teams.
- Keep the public/private application boundary intact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Responsive layouts, semantic navigation, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support and accessible audio controls.
